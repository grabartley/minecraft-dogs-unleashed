# Dogs Unleashed

Dogs Unleashed adds six dog breeds to Minecraft. Each one has its own look, coats, voice and home biome, and is fully
animated. You can tame them, give them commands, play fetch, put them to bed, and raise families of them.

> **Alpha:** Dogs Unleashed is in alpha and we would love your feedback.
> [Open an issue](https://github.com/grabartley/minecraft-dogs-unleashed/issues/new) and tell us what you think.

## The breeds

| Breed                | Where to find one                         | Coats                     |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Husky**            | Snowy biomes, ice spikes and frozen peaks | 6, plus 4 eye combinations |
| **Dachshund**        | Plains and meadows                        | 10                        |
| **Beagle**           | Forests and meadows                       | 12                        |
| **Golden Retriever** | Beaches                                   | 1, the classic golden     |
| **Shiba Inu**        | Cherry groves                             | 3: red, black and sesame  |
| **Dire Wolf**        | Packs in the coldest peaks and ice fields | 6, from smoke grey to albino white |

Breeds differ in size, health, speed and bite. Some coats are rarer than others. Friendly strays also wander into
villages, and the wandering trader sometimes sells a puppy in a basket.

The dire wolf is the rare one. It roams the coldest peaks in packs that hunt together and never forgive a player who
hurts one of them. Earn a pup's trust and it grows into a wolf the size of a horse that you can ride.

## What you can do

- **Give real commands.** Right-click your dog to open a command wheel: Follow, Heel, Stay, Sit, Hunt, Guard, Free Roam
and Go to Bed. Or sneak and blow its whistle at something to send it there, set it on a mob, or have it fetch a dropped
item. Hunting dogs leave your other pets, villagers and golems alone, but name your farm animals to keep them safe.
- **Follow their nose.** Hand your dog a mob drop, a raw ore or a brush and it leads you to the nearest mob, ore or dig
site that matches. Show it a treasure map and it digs at the X, or a trail ruins sherd and it finds the ruins.
Beagles can smell twice as far. Or send it off exploring from the wheel's Actions, and it comes back
next morning with a find and a map of where it went. Give it saddlebags and it can carry items to
another player, even in another dimension, then trot back home to you.
- **Play fetch.** Throw a stick, a tennis ball or a frisbee. Your dog chases it, picks it up and brings it back to you.
Give a roaming or staying dog a bone and it buries it, then digs it up a few days later and brings it back, sometimes
with a find. Foxes sneak in at night to steal buried bones, and a dog nearby barks and chases them off.
Dogs bark at pillagers too, and earn a rosette for helping you win a raid.
The more time you spend together, the closer your dog grows, from Stranger to Devoted.
- **Give them a bed.** Craft a dog bed or a dog house in any colour and assign it. Your dog goes to bed by itself at
night. Dire wolves are too big for either and nap on the ground instead, once by day and once by night.
Set out a food bowl and a water bowl too, and a hurt dog will eat or drink there while you're away. Hoppers can keep
the food bowl topped up.
- **Kit them out.** Open their gear from the command wheel. Craft them armour from leather, iron, gold, diamond or netherite, or use wolf armour. Your tamed wolves can wear it too. It takes hits for them, cracks as it wears, and mends with its own material. Dye leather armour and their collar any colour, and feed them treats for a short speed and damage boost. Hang a pendant on their collar to keep a mob family away from you both, shield them from fire, falls or drowning, heal you while they're near, or save them from death once. Dress them up in a hat, a bandana, a bow, a mob head or a carved pumpkin, or put them in one of seventeen mob costumes, from a spider or a creeper to an ender dragon. Dye the sheep costume any colour. Saddlebags let them carry things for you.
- **Keep them groomed.** Coats grow out over time, and a long coat slows a dog down. Trim it with shears to collect dog fur. Go
carefully, or your dog ends up with a bad hair day until it grows back.
- **Never lose them.** Dogs on Follow or Heel come with you through portals and long teleports. The rest stay where you left them. If you die, they guard your things and call out when you're back. Bind a dog whistle to call one from anywhere, or open the
Pet Manager to see and summon your whole pack.
- **Raise a family.** Any two living tamed dogs can have puppies, and mixed parents give a mixed breed puppy. Dire wolves only breed with dire wolves. Puppies inherit
their parents' coat and eye genes, so two parents can carry a rare coat and pass it on. Every dog has a family tree you can explore.
Turn on Dog Aging and your dogs grow old with you, going grey around the muzzle and napping more, but never dying of age.
- **Bring them back.** A tamed dog that dies leaves a grave. Put a totem in it and a lightning rod above it, then wait
for lightning to strike it. Your dog comes back undead and burns in daylight unless it has shade, armour, or a fire pendant. Cure it the same way you
cure a zombie villager. If it dies while undead, it is gone for good, unless it wears a Pendant of Loyalty.
Carve any of your dogs, living or lost, into a stone, gold or copper statue with its name on the front, or paint its portrait on a canvas to hang on your wall.
- **Little touches.** Huskies and dire wolves howl at the full moon, dogs tilt their heads at food, and they shake off after rain or a
swim. Muddy dogs leave paw prints until you wash them in a water cauldron. There is also an advancement tab to work through.

## Install

1. Install Fabric Loader, then put Dogs Unleashed, [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) and [GeckoLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/geckolib)
in your `mods` folder.
2. Load a world and find a dog in its home biome.
3. Tame it with meat or a bone, and give it a name.

Dogs Unleashed works in singleplayer and on servers, on Minecraft `1.21.1`. The only thing to set up is the Pet Manager
key. It has no key by default, so it never takes one you already use. Set it under `Options > Controls > Key Binds`, in
the Dogs Unleashed section.

## Settings

Open settings from the cog in the Pet Manager, or from Mod Menu. In singleplayer you can change them. On a server,
only operators can.

<details>
<summary><b>All settings</b></summary>

| Setting                   | Default | What it does                                                                      |
|---------------------------|---------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Spawn Dogs in the Wild    | On      | Whether dogs spawn in their biomes, in new chunks and near you. Biome spawns change after a world restart. |
| Spawn Rate                | 100%    | How many dogs spawn, from 0% to 500%. 200% is about twice as many. Biome spawns change after a world restart. |
| Spawn Rate (per breed)    | 100%    | The same, for one breed. It multiplies with Spawn Rate.                           |
| Spawn Near Players        | On      | Keeps wild dogs of every breed turning up near you, even in well-explored areas.  |
| Village Strays            | On      | Whether stray dogs turn up in villages, two at most per village. Feed one to win it over. |
| Trader Puppies            | On      | Whether the wandering trader sometimes sells a Puppy Basket with a tamed puppy inside. |
| Dog Graves on Death       | On      | Whether a tamed dog leaves a grave when it dies. Its gear drops either way.                        |
| Drop Leash on Play Mode   | On      | Off keeps the lead on, and fetch waits until you take it off.                     |
| Show Dog Names            | On      | Shows or hides name tags above dogs.                                              |
| Muddy Dogs                | On      | Whether dogs get muddy and leave paw prints. Off keeps every dog clean.           |
| Auto-Sleep at Night       | On      | Whether dogs go to bed by themselves.                                             |
| Auto-Sleep Range (blocks) | 32      | How far from its bed a dog can be and still go to sleep, from 4 to 128.           |
| Bark Volume               | 1.0     | How loud barks are, from 0.0 to 2.0.                                              |
| Howl Volume               | 1.5     | How loud howls are, from 0.0 to 2.0.                                              |
| Fur Growth                | On      | Whether coats grow out. Off keeps every coat as it is. Shears still work.          |
| Fur Growth Speed          | 100%    | How fast coats grow, from 0% to 500%.                                             |
| Bad Haircut Chance        | 100%    | How often a trim goes wrong, from 0% to 500%. 0% means every trim is clean.       |
| Dog Aging                 | Off     | Whether adult dogs grow old. Seniors go grey around the muzzle, slow down a little and nap more. They never die of age. |
| Senior Age (days)         | 100     | How many in-game days an adult lives before it becomes a senior, from 1 to 1000.  |
| Dire Wolf Growth Speed    | 100%    | How fast dire wolves grow up, from 0% to 500%. 0% stops them growing.             |
| Alpha Battles             | 20      | Fights a prime must win beside you before it can become an alpha, from 1 to 200.  |
| Smallest / Largest Pack   | 5 / 10  | How many dire wolves every wild pack has, from 4 to 16.                           |
| Wild Packs Hunt           | On      | Whether wild dire wolves hunt animals, skeletons and zombies. Penned animals are safe. |
| Packs Defend Each Other   | On      | Whether a wild pack attacks anything that hurts one of them, and goes after players it holds a grudge against. |
| Riding Stamina Drain      | On      | Off lets you sprint on a dire wolf for as long as you like.                       |
| Riding Stamina            | 100%    | How long a dire wolf can sprint, from 25% to 400%.                                |
| Stamina Recovery          | 100%    | How fast a dire wolf gets its stamina back, from 25% to 400%.                     |

</details>

<summary><b>Commands</b></summary>

Every subcommand needs operator permission.

```
/dogsunleashed status
/dogsunleashed config spawn <true|false>

/dogsunleashed config spawnrate <breed> <0..500>
/dogsunleashed config capindependentspawning <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config villagestrays <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config traderpuppies <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config graves <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config dropleashonplay <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config shownames <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config muddydogs <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config autosleep <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config autosleeprange <4..128>
/dogsunleashed config barkvolume <0.0..2.0>
/dogsunleashed config howlvolume <0.0..2.0>
/dogsunleashed config furgrowth <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config furgrowthrate <0..500>
/dogsunleashed config badhairdaychance <0..500>
/dogsunleashed config dogaging <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config senioragedays <1..1000>
/dogsunleashed config direwolf growthrate <0..500>
/dogsunleashed config direwolf battles <1..200>
/dogsunleashed config direwolf smallestpack <4..16>
/dogsunleashed config direwolf largestpack <4..16>
/dogsunleashed config direwolf packhunting <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config direwolf packdefence <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config direwolf staminadrain <true|false>
/dogsunleashed config direwolf staminacapacity <25..400>
/dogsunleashed config direwolf staminarecovery <25..400>
/dogsunleashed config reset
```

The breed ids are `husky`, `dachshund`, `beagle`, `goldenretriever`, `shibainu` and `direwolf`. `list`, `summon` and `find` let

## Languages

Dogs Unleashed is in English, Simplified Chinese (简体中文), German (Deutsch), French (Français), Portuguese (Português do
Brasil), Russian (Русский) and Spanish (Español).

Want to add your language? Copy [`en_us.json`](src/main/resources/assets/dogs-unleashed/lang/en_us.json), translate the
text, and open a pull request. Keep the keys and the `%s` placeholders as they are, and leave command names and
arguments untranslated. Found a mistake in a translation?
[Open an issue](https://github.com/grabartley/minecraft-dogs-unleashed/issues/new).
